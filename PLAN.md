The over pathing/guide is based on the following guide:

https://eldenring.redmaw.dev/sheets/walkthrough

Some of the differences are that my companion would walk you through every piece of content there is. The website talks about what isn't included in the FAQ, but also I want every piece of dialogue, every encounter, every unique item, etc. It would take multiple playthroughs to do this so the companion would track that. 

It would also include locations to best farm every crafting item as well as rune farms, though it doesn't rely on the farms for the player leveling and should rely on just progressing through the game.

The companion should also have a character builder similar to this:

https://www.tarnished.dev/build-planner

There are example builds in there that are some of the more common builds. The end product I wanted would give the best armors and weapons for the build as you progress through the level and game naturally based on what you would have come across at that point. This way, as you level up and explore more, you can try new stuff

There's also a ranked list of all the items, talismans, weapons, etc. This may need to get updated from 1.17. Not sure if there are updated rankings but I get most of my data from a few youtubers. You can probably just carry them over from the ones I had in the previous version

There should also be a lore companion that, as you progress, unlocks more and more lore material so you can experience an event and then read about it with full spoilers, but only after you experience it (minimizing spoilers)

All achievements should be completed. There are 6 unique endings with 3 of them leading to achievements. It is possible to be able to experience them all depending on how you play the game. You'd need to do some saving manipulation to experience them all in a single playthrough but it's not hard. The hard part is having them all available (hence the guide).

There should be an option to view your collection as you progress. For example, if a step has you go somewhere to get an item, when that step is marked complete, it should be marked off on a "master collection" display. A lot of weapons and armor must be farmed, so a way to track these as well would need to be included.
